How it works

From first instruction to final account

  1. Take on the client

    Capture the client, run a conflict check and complete FICA before any work starts.

  2. Open the matter

    Assign the attorney and team, set the key dates and add the tasks for that type of matter.

  3. Do the work

    Diary entries, documents, emails and time are all recorded on the matter.

  4. Bill and close

    Invoice the recorded work, reconcile trust and close the file.

Why CounselNexus

Made for South African legal practice

Overseas systems are often not set up for local rules. In CounselNexus, trust accounting, FICA, POPIA and prescription are part of each matter.

Trust accounting

Ledgers, reconciliations and reports set up for South African trust account rules.

FICA

Identification, verification and risk assessment kept with the client.

POPIA

Consent, retention and access requests, with a record of who did what.

Prescription and dies

Dies and prescription dates are calculated, and you are reminded before they run out.
