How it works

From first instruction to final account

  1. Take on the client

    Capture the client once, run the conflict check and complete FICA before work begins.

  2. Open the matter

    Assign the team, set key dates and let the right tasks and documents follow.

  3. Do the work

    Diary, documents, emails and time stay together, so nothing falls through the cracks.

  4. Bill and close

    Turn recorded work into accurate invoices, reconcile trust and close the file cleanly.
