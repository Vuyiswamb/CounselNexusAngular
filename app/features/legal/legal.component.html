Cookie Policy

The cookies and browser storage used by the CounselNexus website and system.

Home / Cookie Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website saves in your browser. Websites can also use similar browser storage, such as local storage, to remember information between visits. In this policy we call all of these "cookies".

2. Cookies we use

  • Needed to sign in. When you sign in to CounselNexus, your browser stores a sign in token and your basic user details so that you stay signed in as you move between pages. These are removed when you sign out. The system cannot work without them.
  • Remember me. If you tick "Remember me" on the sign in page, your browser stores your email address so it is filled in next time. Untick the box to remove it.
  • Website chat. The Zoho SalesIQ chat on our website sets its own cookies to keep your chat session going between pages and to recognise you if you return. Zoho may also collect information about your browser and device. See Zoho's privacy and cookie policies for details.
  • Web fonts. Our pages load the Poppins font from Google Fonts. When this happens your browser connects to Google, which receives your IP address and browser details.

3. Cookies we do not use

We do not use advertising cookies, and we do not currently use analytics or tracking cookies of our own. If we add them later, we will update this policy and ask for your consent where it is required.

4. Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Most browsers also let you clear local storage. If you block or clear the cookies that are needed to sign in, you will be signed out and will not be able to use the CounselNexus system until you allow them again. Blocking the chat cookies will stop the website chat from working, but the rest of the website will still work.

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when we change the cookies we use. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.

6. Contact us

Questions about cookies can be sent to info@counselnexus.co.za or +27 12 007 2568. See our Privacy Policy for more on how we handle personal information.
